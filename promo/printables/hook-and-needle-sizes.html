Free Crochet Hook and Knitting Needle Size Chart

Hooks and needles are sized three ways: metric millimeters, US letters and numbers, and the old UK and Canadian numbers on vintage tools and patterns. This chart lines them all up on one page.

Download the free PDF A4 paper size

One page. Prints well in black and white.

On the sheet

  • Crochet hooks from 2.25 mm to 25 mm
  • Knitting needles from 2 mm to 25 mm
  • Notes where charts disagree, so nothing catches you out

Always check the mm size

Letters and numbers can differ between brands. Some brands make a 4.25 mm G hook, for example, so the millimeter size is the one to trust.

Vintage needles and patterns

Old UK and Canadian needle sizes run backwards: the bigger the number, the thinner the needle. An old UK 8 is a 4 mm needle, which is a US 6.
