Free US to UK Crochet Terms Chart

Crochet patterns are written in US or UK terms, and the two use the same names for different stitches. This one page chart puts them side by side, both ways, so you can follow any pattern with confidence.

Why the names are different

The stitches are the same in both systems. Only the names change. Each UK stitch name is one step up from the US name for the same stitch, so a UK double crochet (dc) is a US single crochet (sc), and a UK treble (tr) is a US double crochet (dc).

Which terms does my pattern use?

  • If you see single crochet (sc) or half double crochet (hdc), it is a US pattern. UK terms do not have those stitches.
  • Canadian patterns use US terms. Australian patterns use UK terms.
  • US patterns talk about gauge. UK patterns call it tension.
