Free Crochet and Knitting Charts to Print

Six one page cheat sheets for your project bag: US and UK terms, abbreviations, hook and needle sizes, yarn weights and a row counter. Each one is free to print, in US Letter or A4.

  • US and UK crochet termsBoth directions, with a stitch ladder and three ways to tell which terms a pattern uses.
  • Crochet abbreviations43 common abbreviations in US terms, with the UK word where it differs.
  • Knitting abbreviations53 common abbreviations in plain words, plus the words UK patterns use.
  • Hook and needle sizesMetric, US and old UK and Canadian sizes for hooks and needles.
  • Yarn weight chartWeights 0 to 7 with US, UK and Australian names, gauge and tool sizes.
  • Row counter and tracker120 row boxes, repeat circles, stitch count checks and notes.

Counting rows? Stitch Scroll keeps your place.

Stitch Scroll reads the knitting or crochet pattern you own and turns it into rows you count with a tap. It checks the stitch counts row by row before you start, so a slip in the pattern shows up early. Every row shows how many stitches you should end with.
