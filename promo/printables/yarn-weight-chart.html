Free Printable Yarn Weight Chart

Lace, fingering, DK, worsted, aran, chunky: yarn weights go by different names in different countries. This chart lines up the standard weights from 0 to 7 with their US, UK and Australian names, plus the gauge and tool sizes that usually suit them.

Download the free PDF A4 paper size

One page. Prints well in black and white.

On the sheet

  • All 8 standard weights, from 0 to 7
  • US, UK and Australian names side by side
  • Typical knit and crochet gauge, with needle and hook sizes

Swapping a yarn

Start with the weight number, then compare gauge. The ranges on the chart are typical, and the gauge in your pattern always comes first, so make a swatch before you buy enough for the whole project.

Names around the world

A US worsted or aran is a UK aran and an Australian 10 ply. A US DK is a UK DK and an Australian 8 ply.
