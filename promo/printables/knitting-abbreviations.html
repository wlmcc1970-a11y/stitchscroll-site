Free Knitting Abbreviations Printable

From k2tog to w&t, knitting patterns are full of short forms. This one page cheat sheet spells out 53 of the most common ones in plain words, so you can keep knitting instead of searching.

Download the free PDF A4 paper size

On the sheet

  • 53 common abbreviations in plain words
  • Which increases and decreases lean left or right
  • Words that differ in UK patterns, like cast off for bind off

Left and right leaning

Some increases and decreases lean one way. k2tog leans right and ssk leans left. M1R leans right and M1L leans left. The sheet marks each one.

Knitting from a UK pattern

UK patterns say cast off instead of bind off, stocking stitch instead of stockinette stitch, and tension instead of gauge.
