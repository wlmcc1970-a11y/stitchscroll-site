How it works

  1. Photograph your pattern

    Take a photo of the pages, or choose photos or a PDF. One Scan Credit reads up to 5 pages of instructions. Your first scan asks for a free sign-in with Apple or Google.

  2. Check it before you start

    Stitch Scroll checks the stitch counts row by row and shows you anything worth a second look. It never fills in a line it could not read.

  3. Count with one tap

    Tap Row done and Stitch Scroll moves you to the next row or round, with the designer's own printed line beside it.

Get Stitch Scroll

Stitch Scroll reads the knitting or crochet pattern you own and turns it into rows you count with a tap. It checks the stitch counts row by row before you start, so a slip in the pattern shows up early. Tap any stitch, like sc or inc, to see what it means.

Scanning your own pattern needs a free sign-in with Apple or Google when you first scan. The sample patterns, the Stitch Guide and the free printable charts work without an account.
