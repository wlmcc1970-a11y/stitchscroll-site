How it works

  1. Take a photo of your pattern

    Photograph each page, or choose photos or a PDF. 1 Scan Credit = up to 5 pages. Your first scan asks for a free sign-in with Apple or Google.

  2. Check it before you start

    Stitch Scroll checks each stitch count against the row before it. Anything worth a second look is shown to you first, and you decide.

  3. Count with one tap

    Tap Row done and Stitch Scroll moves you to the next row or round, with the designer's own printed line beside it.

Questions

What does Stitch Scroll do?

You take a photo of the knitting or crochet pattern you own, or choose photos or a PDF. Stitch Scroll turns it into rows you count with one tap, with the designer's own printed line beside every row.

What kinds of patterns does it read?

Printed patterns, PDF patterns and clearly handwritten notes, for knitting and crochet, from amigurumi to hats, blankets and dishcloths. Stitch Scroll reads only the pattern you give it and never makes up a pattern.

How do Scan Credits work?

3 Scan Credits are included and are added the first time you sign in. 1 Scan Credit = up to 5 pages of instructions. Cover and photo pages are free. Need more? Scan Credits come in packs of 1, 5 or 10, and they never expire.

Do I need an account?

Scanning your own pattern needs a free sign-in, right when you first scan. The 4 sample patterns, the Stitch Guide and the printable charts work without an account.

What happens to my pattern?

Your patterns, page photos and counts are never sold. Your pages are sent to our reading service only to be read. When you are signed in, your patterns sync between your devices, and you can delete your account any time in Settings. Read the privacy policy.

What if a line is hard to read?

Before you start, Stitch Scroll checks each stitch count against the row before it. Crossed-out numbers, notes in the margin and lines that could mean two things are shown to you first, and a line it finds hard to read is marked for you to check against your page.

Is there a subscription or ads?

No. You buy the app once. There are no ads and no subscription.

Is Stitch Scroll on iPhone?

Stitch Scroll is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets. If you want it on iPhone, join the list and we will write once if it comes to iPhone.
