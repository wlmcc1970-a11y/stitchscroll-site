Never lose your place in a pattern again.

Stitch Scroll is a knitting and crochet pattern reader and row counter for Android. You take a photo of the pattern you own, and it turns it into rows you count with one tap, with the designer's own printed line beside every row.

The honest limits.

  • Scanning needs an internet connection. The reading happens on a server, not on the phone. Counting, your saved patterns, the Stitch Guide and the printables work without it.
  • Handwriting has to be clear. A line it finds hard to read is marked for you to check against your page.
  • It does not guess. When a stitch count is not certain, it is left off instead of guessed. Crossed-out numbers and lines that could mean two things are shown to you first, and you decide.
  • Long patterns use more credits. 1 Scan Credit = up to 5 pages of instructions. Cover and photo pages are free.
  • Android only for now. People who want it on iPhone can join the list on the home page.

I would rather you knew all of this going in than ran into it mid-review.

It reads. It does not invent.

Stitch Scroll uses an AI model to read the photos of a pattern you already own and turn its words and charts into rows. That is the only thing the technology does inside the app. It never makes up a pattern, and it reads only the pages you choose. Your patterns, page photos and counts are never sold. Exactly how your data is handled is in the Stitch Scroll Privacy Policy, and you can delete your account from inside the app.
